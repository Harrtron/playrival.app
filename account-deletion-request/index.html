Delete your account

RIVAL: Football Fan Games · Developer: Play The Future (Play The Future Ltd)

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and personal data for RIVAL: Football Fan Games, the mobile app published on Google Play by Play The Future.

How to request account deletion

Follow these steps to ask us to delete your account and associated data:

RIVAL accounts are linked to your mobile phone number only — we do not use email addresses for sign-in or account identification.

  1. Use the button below to open a pre-filled email to support@playrival.app with the subject “Account Deletion Request - RIVAL: Football Fan Games”.
  2. Include your registered phone number (including country code) — the same number you use to sign in to RIVAL. We use this to locate and verify your account.
  3. Optionally include your RIVAL username if you know it, to help us find your account faster.
  4. Confirm that you want your account permanently deleted. We will verify your identity using your registered phone number, process the request, and reply to your support email once deletion is complete.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send your request manually to support@playrival.app with the subject line above.

What data is deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we delete or anonymise personal data tied to your account, including:

  • Your account credentials and registered mobile phone number
  • Profile information you added (such as username and profile details)
  • Gameplay data linked to you (predictions, scores, leaderboard entries, match participation, in-game actions, and related timestamps)
  • Friend connections and address-book matching data associated with your account
  • Real-time session and presence data linked to your account
  • Device identifiers and diagnostic data stored in our systems that can identify you personally

What data may be kept

Some information may be retained after account deletion, as described below:

  • Aggregated or anonymised analytics that can no longer be used to identify you (for example, overall feature usage statistics).
  • Security and fraud-prevention logs (such as IP addresses and authentication events) retained for up to 90 days to protect the Services and other users.
  • Backup copies of deleted account data in our systems, which are purged on a rolling basis within up to 30 days after deletion is processed.
  • Support correspondence if you previously contacted us, kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and for our legitimate record-keeping (typically up to 2 years).
  • Information we must keep by law, such as records required for tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations (retained for the period required by applicable law, which may be up to 6 years in the United Kingdom).
  • Content already visible to other players (for example, usernames or scores shown in past match results) may remain in anonymised or de-identified form where removing it would affect the integrity of historical gameplay for other users.

Processing time

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may need additional time where backups, legal obligations, or dispute resolution require limited retention. We will reply to your support request when your account has been deleted.

For more detail on how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy. For other account help, visit Support.
