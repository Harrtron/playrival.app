Every other football app is paid for by bookmakers.

That's why they all look the same, and why they're all pointed at what happens next. There's no bet in a match from 1997, so nobody builds for it. The apps that do go deep on history are web-first, clunky and funded by ads, most of them gambling ads.

We don't take bookmaker money. Fans pay for RIVAL, so fans are the only people we answer to. No odds on screen. No gambling ads. Nothing in the product pushing you toward a bet.
