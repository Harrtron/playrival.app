# RIVAL Brand Positioning Spec
**Version 1.0 — April 2026 · Internal Use Only**

---

## 01 The Brand Truth

Sport was always meant to be felt this deeply. RIVAL just makes sure it is.

A generation hasn't lost love for sport. They've lost patience with being passive inside it. They grew up with games, with stakes, with skin in everything they do. Sitting on the outside of a match — watching it happen to someone else — doesn't work for them anymore.

RIVAL doesn't compete with the match. It lives inside it. Every prediction, every act of chaos, every round is a reason to care about the next minute. The match generates the game. The game makes the match impossible to ignore.

We are not anti-watching. We are pro-feeling. The more invested a player is in RIVAL, the more invested they are in the match. That's the loop. That's the mission.

---

## 02 Mission Statement

**From spectator to rival.**

The emotive mission line. Spectator is what you were — present but passive. Rival is what RIVAL makes you. The destination identity is built into the brand name.

Supporting mission context for longer-form brand copy: RIVAL exists to close the gap between the fan in the stands and the game on the pitch. Not by replacing the match — but by giving every fan a live stake in every minute of it. We're building a generation of rivals, not spectators.

## 03 Taglines & Where They Live

### Primary — use everywhere
**Step out of the stands.**
The hero brand line. An invitation, not a confrontation. You're still at the game — just somewhere more alive. Works pre-match, in-match, on every surface. Carries the spatial transformation without dismissing the love of watching.

### Mission / About
**From spectator to rival.**
The transformation in four words. Used in brand story contexts — website about section, investor comms, press.

### Conversion / Social / Ads
**Rival everyone.**
Brand name as verb. Competition as identity. The CTA and the tagline are the same thing. Use to drive download, registration, and match entry.

### Community / Movement
**The fans who play.**
Identity statement for the RIVAL community. Not users. Not viewers. The fans who play. Used in community comms, early adopter framing, season launches.

### Onboarding / First match
**This is your game now.**
The moment of transformation. Used at the point someone enters their first lobby. Creates the shift from passive fan to active rival.

### In-app / Live moments
**No dead time.**
The product promise in three words. Every second has stakes. Used in live match contexts, round transitions, and feature copy.

### Influencer / Watchalong
**The game the match creates.**
Poetic and true — RIVAL doesn't exist without the match. Best for influencer briefs and creator content where there's space for nuance.

## 04 What RIVAL Is and Isn't

This distinction must be maintained across all copy. RIVAL sits in a unique position — not betting, not fantasy, not a quiz. Getting this wrong in copy damages both credibility and App Store positioning.

| What we're not | What we are |
|---|---|
| A betting app | A live multiplayer game — no money changes hands. Predictions are skill-based. No gambling language anywhere. |
| A quiz app | A competitive live game — questions are the mechanic, not the product. The rivalry, leaderboard, chaos — that's the product. |
| Fantasy sport | The game inside the live match — live, minute-to-minute, inside the 90 minutes. |
| Anti-watching | Pro-immersion — RIVAL makes watching better. Never position as a replacement for the match. |

## 05 Brand Voice

- **Direct** — Say the thing. No preamble. No filler. Every word earns its place or it's cut.
- **Alive** — Present tense. Active voice. Copy should feel like it's happening now, not being described after the fact.
- **Competitive** — Rivalry is the brand. Copy should carry the energy of someone who wants to win — but never at the expense of welcome.
- **Collective** — "We" over "you" where possible. RIVAL is a movement. The lobby, the community, the season — all of it is shared.
- **Confident** — No hedging. No "kind of" or "sort of". RIVAL knows what it is. The copy should too.
- **Never corporate** — No "gamified experience". No "second screen engagement". No investor language in user-facing copy. Ever.

## 06 Words We Use / Words We Don't

### Use
- Rivals — not users or players
- The RIVAL Elite — not early adopters
- The lobby — not a game session
- The match — not the game (that's RIVAL)
- Chaos — owned, deliberate
- Season — not period or phase
- Step out of the stands
- The game the match creates
- Rival everyone
- No dead time

### Don't use
- Gamified — describes tech, not feeling
- Second screen — investor language
- Passive viewing — too clinical
- Users — too product, not community
- Predict — sounds like betting
- Platform — too corporate
- Engagement — marketing speak
- Interactive experience — meaningless
- Never a dull game — cliché
- Don't watch, play — anti-watching

## 07 Channel Application

### Website — Hero
**Step out of the stands.** — then one line: "RIVAL is the live multiplayer game played inside every match. Create chaos. Rival everyone in your lobby."
CTA: **Join The RIVAL Elite.**

### App Store — Title & Subtitle
- Title: RIVAL — Live Sport Game
- Subtitle: Step out of the stands.
- First line of description: "The live multiplayer game you play inside every match."

### Onboarding
Open with the identity shift: Step out of the stands. Walk through the loop in one sentence per step. Close with belonging: Welcome to The RIVAL Elite. Season 1 starts now.

### In-App — Live moments
Short. Present tense. Active.
- "The match is live. Your lobby is waiting."
- "Round 3 is live."
- "You're 2 correct from the title."
- "Drop a card. Change everything."
Never passive, never explanatory during live play.

### Influencer Brief
Frame: "I play RIVAL during every match — come watch and play with me." The product is the content. The watchalong is the activation. RIVAL should feel like something they discovered, not something they're promoting. Key line for influencers: **The game the match creates.**

### Social / Organic
Community-first. Highlight moments from lobbies — chaos landing, last-second wins, leaderboard drama. Caption register: direct, short, competitive. Always end on rivalry: **Rival everyone.**

### Pre-Match Content
Build anticipation around the lobby, not just the match. "The match kicks off in 30. Your lobby is waiting." Pre-match is where RIVAL earns the habit — make it feel like part of the match ritual.

## 08 The Movement Frame

RIVAL is not an app people use. It's something people are part of. The language of early membership matters enormously for Gen Z/A — being first is cultural currency.

> The fans who got here first are building what RIVAL becomes.

Use this frame in all early-adopter and The RIVAL Elite communications. Season 1 players are pioneers, not beta testers.

Community identity terms: The RIVAL community. The RIVAL Elite. Season 1. The lobby. Your rivals. The league. These are not feature names. They are identity markers that create belonging and signal to newcomers that there is something worth joining.

## 09 Match MVP

On the pitch, there's a player of the match. In your lobby, it's you.

Match MVP is the pinnacle moment in RIVAL — the identity stamp that comes from winning a lobby. It is permanent, specific, and undeniable. Not a badge in a collection. A record on a match.

**The line:** Match MVP — sealed with the score, the date, the moment. You own that match forever.

Use "put a name on it" as the frame for MVP copy. The win is personal. The record is public. The ownership is forever.

Match MVP sits alongside Rival Leagues (earn RP, climb divisions, get promoted) and Ref Rating (a rolling score of how sharp your calls really are) as the three pillars of progression in RIVAL. Together they answer: who won tonight, who's climbing the season, and how good are you really.
